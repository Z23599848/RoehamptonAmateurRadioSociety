# Before you transmit

**RARS classroom reminder | Week 2**

This is a prompt for checking the applicable rules, not a replacement for the licence or a complete live-station safety assessment.

| Check | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| Operator | Who is operating, and what valid licence or authorised supervision covers them? |
| Identity | Whose call sign applies? Is any RSL correct for the station's location? |
| Place | Is this location within the licence's authority? Do ship/aircraft permissions or restrictions apply? |
| Frequency | Is the full transmitted signal within an allowed allocation? Have I read the row's restrictions? |
| Power | What limit applies here, and is it expressed as PEP, ERP or EIRP? |
| Purpose | Is the communication appropriate for amateur radio? Who is the intended recipient? |
| Settings | Are mode, bandwidth and operating arrangements suitable? |
| Sharing | Have I listened and checked the applicable band plan and coordination requirements? |
| Safety | Have relevant electrical, antenna and EMF checks been completed? |
| Control | Can operation be stopped promptly? If supervised, is the responsible supervisor present? |

During the contact, keep the station identifiable and remain alert to interference. If supervision, settings or circumstances change, reassess before continuing.

**If you cannot explain a check, pause and ask.** A frequency that sounds quiet, a low power setting or membership of a society is not sufficient permission on its own.

Use this checklist for role-play this week. Detailed technical and safety preparation continues in later modules.
