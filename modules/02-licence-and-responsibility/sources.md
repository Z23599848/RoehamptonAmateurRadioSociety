## Editorial decisions

- No second Essex Ham transcript was supplied. This is original RARS teaching based directly on the official references, not a claim to have watched or transcribed another video.
- Call signs in the exercises are illustrative. They must not be adopted as actual transmitting identities.
- Identification examples distinguish the general licence obligation from specific changes highlighted in the exam syllabus. No fixed 15-minute rule has been invented.
- The guidance document's section 3.4 says "ERP" for the airborne 500 mW figure, while the formal OFW611 Schedule 1 Table A and EX307 specify **EIRP**. The lesson follows the formal conditions and exam reference: EIRP. These qualifiers are not interchangeable.
- The 431-432 MHz restriction is taught as a location exclusion, not a lower power allowance. Roehampton falls within the 100 km radius around Charing Cross.
- The three table examples do not constitute an authorisation for a live activity. The classroom session is non-transmitting.
- Quiz questions, scenarios and the station checklist are newly authored, not copied examination questions. Official documents are linked rather than redistributed in the pack.

## Tutor review before delivery

Check the reference versions, work every table exercise, and read all model answers. Revisit partial items before declaring licensing coverage complete. For actual station operation, the current licence and applicable coordination requirements control; a course summary or specimen exam band plan is not a substitute.
