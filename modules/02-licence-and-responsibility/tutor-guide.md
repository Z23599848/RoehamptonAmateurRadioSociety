# Week 2 tutor guide

**Your licence and responsible operation | 90 minutes**

The aim is to turn rules into sensible decisions. Use discussion and role-play, not live transmitting. Read the learner guide and source notes before delivery.

## Prepare

- Provide the current EX307 reference booklet, worksheet and quiz.
- Have the current Ofcom conditions available for questions; the course summary is not the licence itself.
- Put the seven individual RSLs on a board or printed cards.
- Use a disconnected microphone or ordinary spoken role-play for supervision examples.
- Label all sample call signs as illustrations. Do not invent an RARS club call sign or assume the society already holds a Full (Club) licence.

If the group needs longer on table reading, review the remaining quiz answers as homework. Do not squeeze an unplanned live contact into the remaining time.

## Prompts and likely misconceptions

**"I passed, so I can transmit."** Ask which organisation issues the licence. Have learners say the separate steps aloud.

**"I only update my address every five years."** Put a house move and periodic revalidation on different cards. Ask what triggers each action.

**"The operator lives in Wales, so W always applies."** Move the imaginary station to Scotland. The optional RSL follows the operating location.

**"I borrowed a better radio, so I have more power."** Change the equipment card but leave the operator's licence card in place. The radio does not upgrade the licence.

**"The supervisor is reachable by phone."** Ask whether they can physically take back control. Stop the mock contact and rehearse a proper handover.

**"Every digital signal is encrypted."** Separate representation/encoding from intentional concealment of meaning. Avoid claiming that every digital system is suitable for amateur use.

**"25 W is the Foundation limit everywhere."** Compare 144-146 MHz, 2400-2450 MHz and the restricted London row. Require the full qualifier, not just the number.

**"A quiet channel is permission."** Ask what a silent receiver tells you about the licence. It cannot remove a restriction.

## Assessment and scope

Use 10/12 plus explained corrections as a RARS learning target, not an official threshold. The worksheet checks additional reasoning that a twelve-question quiz cannot cover on its own.

The source map records licensing points taught and remaining coverage. The detailed EMF topic is deliberately deferred to Week 6 and must be taught before claiming full Section 1 coverage. Identification and message handling should be practised again in Week 7.

## Homework - 30 minutes

- 10 minutes: correct the quiz and explain each correction.
- 10 minutes: reproduce the seven RSLs, then check and correct them.
- 5 minutes: repeat the three table lookups and read each restriction aloud.
- 5 minutes: use the station checklist to explain a hypothetical contact to a partner.

Ask the learner to return with one question, especially if a licence phrase is unfamiliar. Offer verbal explanations of the worksheet as well as written answers.
