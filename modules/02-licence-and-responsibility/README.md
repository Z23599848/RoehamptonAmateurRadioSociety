# Week 2 - Your licence and responsible operation

**Roehampton Amateur Radio Society | UK Foundation preparation**

90-minute taught session plus 30 minutes of consolidation. Complete [Week 1](../01-first-steps/README.md) first, or be comfortable with bands, frequencies and modes.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, you should be able to:

1. Explain the difference between an exam pass and an Ofcom licence, and keep licence details current.
2. Recognise regional secondary locators and explain when one is optional or mandatory.
3. Decide when to identify a station and whose call sign applies during supervised operation.
4. Distinguish normal amateur messages, an initial CQ call and prohibited business traffic.
5. Explain why Morse or a digital mode is not automatically encryption.
6. Identify the geographical limits of a Foundation licence and the extra checks for a ship or aircraft.
7. Read frequency, status, power units and restrictions together, including the London exclusion example.
8. Explain interference responsibility, Ofcom's inspection powers and when a log is required.
