# Week 2 knowledge check

**12 original RARS questions | About 8 minutes**

Choose one answer per question. Use the current EX307 booklet for Questions 9 and 10. This is a weekly learning check, not an official exam paper.

**1. Your recorded address changes. Which response follows the licence-maintenance requirements?**

A. Wait until the next five-year check.  
B. Update only a public call-sign website.  
C. Notify Ofcom immediately in writing, and also keep up with periodic revalidation.  
D. No action is needed once the exam has been passed.

**2. Which statement about regional secondary locators is correct?**

A. They are normally optional, but mandatory for Intermediate call signs beginning with 2.  
B. They are compulsory for every Foundation transmission.  
C. They always follow the operator's home address.  
D. They are optional only for Full licensees.

**3. During an ongoing contact, a station changes from FM to a different mode. What is the Foundation syllabus's identification expectation?**

A. Identify only when the operator also changes location.  
B. Identify only if another station asks.  
C. Wait for a fixed 15-minute interval regardless of the change.  
D. Identify the station again when the mode changes.

**4. An unlicensed learner operates under a personal licensee's direct supervision. Which applies?**

A. The learner can invent a temporary call sign.  
B. The supervisor's call sign and licence conditions apply, and the supervisor remains responsible.  
C. Club membership gives the learner Full privileges.  
D. The radio's maximum advertised power sets the permitted limit.

**5. Which use falls outside the normal non-commercial purpose of an amateur licence?**

A. Comparing reception reports  
B. Dispatching a company's paid delivery jobs  
C. Investigating an antenna  
D. A hobby conversation with another amateur

**6. Which is a permitted initial invitation for another amateur station to establish contact?**

A. A commercial advertising broadcast  
B. An unlicensed public music service  
C. A deliberately anonymous transmission  
D. A properly identified CQ call within the applicable operating conditions

**7. Why is ordinary Morse not automatically prohibited encryption?**

A. It is a known code representing characters, not inherently a method of concealing meaning.  
B. Every amateur message is permitted to be encrypted.  
C. Morse cannot carry words.  
D. Licence rules apply only to speech.

**8. A Foundation licensee takes a radio to France. What does the UK Foundation licence itself authorise there?**

A. Unrestricted use of all UK amateur bands  
B. Any transmission below 25 W  
C. It does not itself authorise operation in another country's territory.  
D. Any transmission with `/P` added

**9. Use EX307. A Foundation operator proposes 431.500 MHz from Roehampton, within 100 km of Charing Cross. Which conclusion is correct?**

A. It is always available at 25 W.  
B. It becomes available by selecting FM.  
C. The location restriction makes that allocation unavailable there; reducing power does not remove it.  
D. It is available whenever the frequency sounds quiet.

**10. Use EX307. What maximum PEP is listed for Foundation ordinary terrestrial operation in 2400-2450 MHz?**

A. 100 W  
B. 2 W  
C. 10 W  
D. 25 W

**11. An Ofcom-authorised person requires a specified operating log. Which response is correct?**

A. Keep any informal notes instead of the specified record.  
B. Ignore the instruction because hobby logs are usually voluntary.  
C. Keep a log only if the instruction also closes the station.  
D. Keep the record in the required form and for the required period.

**12. For permitted airborne amateur operation, which statement is correct?**

A. Captain/person-in-control permission is needed, and primary-allocation and 500 mW EIRP restrictions also apply.  
B. The normal terrestrial power limit always applies.  
C. A Foundation licence permits operation over every country.  
D. An optional suffix removes the airborne power limit.
