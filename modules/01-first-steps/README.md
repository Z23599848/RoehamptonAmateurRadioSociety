# Week 1 · Your first steps in amateur radio

**Roehampton Amateur Radio Society · UK Foundation preparation**

90-minute taught session + approximately 30 minutes of independent practice. No previous radio knowledge or equipment purchase is needed.

## What you will learn

By the end, you should be able to:

1. Describe amateur radio and explain its non-commercial purpose.
2. Name the three UK amateur licence levels and distinguish an exam pass from a licence.
3. Classify a frequency as HF, VHF or UHF.
4. Explain frequency and wavelength, including their units and inverse relationship.
5. Use the official conversion chart to estimate a wavelength or frequency.
6. Distinguish a frequency, a band and a mode; recognise common mode names.
7. Find the limits and status of an allocation in the Foundation reference table and explain why spectrum sharing matters.
