# Week 1 knowledge check

**12 original RARS practice questions · approximately 8 minutes**

Choose one answer for each question. Use EX307 for Questions 8 and 12; try the others from memory. This is a weekly learning check, not an official examination paper or a full-syllabus mock.

## Questions

**1. Which proposed activity fits the purpose of an amateur radio licence?**

A. Broadcasting advertisements for a paid service  
B. Allocating paid taxi journeys  
C. Investigating how a different antenna affects a hobby radio contact  
D. Running a commercial dispatch network

**2. Which sequence names the three UK amateur licence levels in the usual progression?**

A. Foundation → Intermediate → Full  
B. Foundation → Professional → Full  
C. Beginner → Foundation → Commercial  
D. Intermediate → Foundation → Full

**3. A signal has a frequency of 14.2 MHz. Which broad spectrum range contains it?**

A. VHF  
B. HF  
C. UHF  
D. None of these

**4. A frequency of 145 MHz means:**

A. A wavelength of 145 metres  
B. 145 cycles per minute  
C. 145,000 cycles per second  
D. 145,000,000 cycles per second

**5. Which description defines wavelength?**

A. The number of complete cycles in one second  
B. The distance occupied by one complete spatial cycle  
C. The number of messages sent in one minute  
D. The strength of the received audio

**6. The frequency doubles and the wave speed stays constant. What happens to wavelength?**

A. It doubles.  
B. It stays the same.  
C. It halves.  
D. It becomes four times as long.

**7. Which broad range contains 435 MHz?**

A. UHF  
B. HF  
C. VHF  
D. The audio range

**8. Use the EX307 conversion chart. Which wavelength is closest to that of a 145 MHz signal?**

A. 20 m  
B. 200 m  
C. 2 m  
D. 0.02 m

**9. Which term describes varying the instantaneous frequency of a carrier to convey information?**

A. AM  
B. SSTV  
C. CW  
D. FM

**10. A handheld radio can tune from 136 to 174 MHz. What does that tell you about your permission to transmit?**

A. Every frequency in that range is available under a Foundation licence.  
B. It describes the equipment's capability; permission must be checked separately.  
C. Any frequency is permitted if nobody answers there.  
D. Frequencies are permitted whenever FM is selected.

**11. What does secondary allocation status mean for an amateur service sharing with a primary service?**

A. It must avoid harmful interference to the primary service and cannot demand protection from that service's legitimate operation.  
B. It has exclusive use after dark.  
C. It can require the primary service to move frequency.  
D. It means only beginners may use the band.

**12. Read the 144–146 MHz row in the current EX307 Foundation table. What is the maximum peak envelope power listed for ordinary terrestrial operation?**

A. 10 W  
B. 100 W  
C. 1,000 W  
D. 25 W
