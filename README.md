# Roehampton Amateur Radio Society

Practical, beginner-friendly learning materials for RARS members preparing for the UK Amateur Radio Foundation examination.

## Start learning

**[Week 1: Your first steps in amateur radio](modules/01-first-steps/README.md)** introduces the hobby, spectrum, wavelength and operating modes.

**[Week 2: Your licence and responsible operation](modules/02-licence-and-responsibility/README.md)** turns licence conditions into practical decisions about call signs, supervision, messages, location and frequency-table restrictions.

Both are complete teaching drafts with a learner guide, worksheet, 90-minute tutor plan, 12 original practice questions and explained answers. Week 1 includes original diagrams; Week 2 adds a before-you-transmit classroom checklist.

## Course status

Version 0.2 | 7 October 2026 | English | Independent RARS teaching material.

The first module was developed from the topics in an Essex Ham introductory transcript supplied by the course organiser, with fresh wording, examples, activities and questions. No second transcript was supplied: Week 2 was newly authored directly from official references. Technical and examination details were checked against current primary sources. This is not an Essex Ham, RSGB or Ofcom publication or endorsement.

The syllabus baseline is **RSGB v1.6b, March 2026 revision**. The linked Foundation reference booklet is **EX307, 22 January 2026**. Tutors should check the [RSGB specifications page](https://rsgb.org/main/clubs-training/for-trainers/syllabus-2019/) and [Ofcom licensing information](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/spectrum/radio-equipment/amateur-radio-info) before each new cohort.

Weeks 1 and 2 cover only part of the syllabus. Detailed EMF and safety teaching is deferred to Week 6; operating practice returns in Week 7. Completing the available modules does not establish readiness for the full examination. The rest of the course and a complete syllabus coverage review are still to be developed.
